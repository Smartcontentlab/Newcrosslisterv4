Features I’d steal from competitors

Here’s what seems to work well across today’s tools:

Feature	Why users like it
Universal listing form	Much faster than re-entering information.  
Automatic sale detection	Prevents double-selling when it works reliably.  
Bulk relist/delist	Essential for older inventory.  
Native mobile app	Sellers photograph and list while sourcing.  
Profit analytics	Helps identify the most profitable brands and categories.  
Multi-marketplace publishing	Saves significant time for high-volume sellers.  

⸻

Features users consistently complain about

These are opportunities for your app to stand out:

* Auto-delisting fails or requires browser windows to stay open.
* Too many features locked behind paid add-ons.
* Slow browser extensions and laggy syncing.
* Weak mobile experience compared with desktop.
* Limited analytics that don’t help improve selling decisions.
* Manual cleanup after imports.
* Poor inventory synchronization across marketplaces.  

⸻

One feature I haven’t seen anyone do well

Since you’re already planning an AI-first app, I’d add an AI Reseller Copilot that stays available everywhere in the interface.

Examples:

* “Why hasn’t this item sold?”
* “What should I price this at?”
* “Rewrite this title for Depop.”
* “Generate an eBay version.”
* “Which marketplace is best for this item?”
* “Show me inventory that’s been sitting over 90 days.”
* “What should I photograph better?”
* “How can I increase my monthly profit by $500?”

Instead of just being a listing tool, your app becomes a business assistant. That would make it much harder to compare directly with existing crosslisting apps, because it would help users improve their resale business—not just post listings.
