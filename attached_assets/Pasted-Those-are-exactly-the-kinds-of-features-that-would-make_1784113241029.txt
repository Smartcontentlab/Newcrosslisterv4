AI Cross-Listing & Reseller Automation Platform

Vision

Build the most intelligent cross-listing platform available by combining AI, marketplace automation, inventory management, pricing intelligence, and seller workflow automation into one easy-to-use dashboard.

The goal is to eliminate repetitive work so sellers can spend more time sourcing inventory and growing their business while AI handles the administrative tasks.

⸻

* Generate detailed descriptions
* Fill item specifics
* Suggest categories
* Estimate shipping weight
* Detect flaws from photos
* Recommend condition
* Generate keywords and tags

Long-Term Goal

Create a fully autonomous resale operating system where AI handles listing creation, pricing research, cross-posting, inventory synchronization, image editing, shipping workflows, and marketplace automation, allowing sellers to operate and scale their business with minimal manual effort.

One feature I’d add because I think it would become a major selling point is an AI Listing Health Score. Every listing would receive a score out of 100 based on factors like title quality, photo quality, pricing competitiveness, description completeness, keyword optimization, shipping cost, and market demand. The system would explain why a listing isn’t performing and offer one-click fixes. That gives users continuous guidance on how to improve sales, not just tools to create listings.
