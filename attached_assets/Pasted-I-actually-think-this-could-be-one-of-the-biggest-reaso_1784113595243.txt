I actually think this could be one of the biggest reasons people pay for the Pro version, but I’d be careful with how it’s designed.

Rather than marketing it as “automation that beats the system” or anything similar, position it as a desktop automation assistant that helps users manage repetitive actions. Keeping it browser-based also makes sense because those workflows are naturally tied to a desktop browser.

Here’s how I’d structure it.

⸻

Automation Hub (Pro)

Overview

The Automation Hub allows users to schedule repetitive marketplace tasks from their desktop browser while monitoring automation status from the mobile app.

Desktop Browser = Configure & Run

Mobile App = Monitor & Control

⸻

Human-like Scheduling

If you’re aiming to make activity look less mechanical, the idea isn’t to make it “undetectable” or bypass platform controls. It’s simply to avoid rigid, perfectly timed schedules.

Instead of:

9:00
10:00
11:00
12:00

Use a scheduling engine that distributes activity across a chosen window.

For example:

Morning Window
9–12
↓
Run sometime within that window
Afternoon
1–4
↓
Run sometime within that window

That creates a more natural schedule than exact hourly intervals.

One idea I think would really differentiate your app

Instead of just saying “Running”, give users confidence that the system is alive by showing a live activity feed:

10:42 AM
Started scheduled task
10:43 AM
Processing...
10:46 AM
Completed successfully
Next scheduled window:
1:00–2:00 PM

That way, if someone opens the mobile app while they’re away from their computer, they immediately know the automation is healthy and active, without needing to remote into their desktop.

One final consideration: if your app includes automation for marketplaces, design those features so users remain in control and the automation operates within each platform’s applicable terms and technical constraints. Building the automation around transparency, scheduling, and status monitoring is a strong value proposition on its own, regardless of which marketplaces you support.
